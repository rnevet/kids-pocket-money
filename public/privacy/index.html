What Pocket Money is

Pocket Money is a family pocket-money ledger. It is a static web application: there is no server operated by the app, no database, and no account system of its own. All data is stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet inside the Google Drive of the parent who created the family.

Data the app accesses

The app requests the Google Drive scope drive.file. This scope lets the app read and write only the spreadsheet it created for your family, or a spreadsheet you explicitly selected through the Google file picker. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.

The app also reads the email address of the signed-in Google account, to record who made each entry and to list family members who have access to the spreadsheet.

Where data is stored

  • Family, kids, transactions and goals: in your own Google Sheets spreadsheet, in your Google Drive.
  • In your browser only (localStorage): the Google access token for the current session, the signed-in email address, the id of the selected spreadsheet, and your language and theme preferences. Signing out clears the token and email.

No data is sent to the operator of this website or to any third party. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing

When a parent invites a family member from the app, the app asks Google Drive to share the spreadsheet with that person's Google account. Google sends the sharing notification email. Access can be removed from the app or from Google Drive at any time.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Pocket Money's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the ledger functionality described above, is never transferred to others, and is never used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your spreadsheet stays in your Drive; delete it there if you no longer want the data.

Contact

Questions about this instance go to the person who set it up and invited you.
